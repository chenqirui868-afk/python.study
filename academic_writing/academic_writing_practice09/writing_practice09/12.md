# Using Excel for Retail Sales Analysis

-Introduction

Data analysis plays a significant role in modern business strategy. For retail companies, understanding sales performance across different stores and products is essential for making informed decisions. In this report, I analyse a simulated dataset from a milk tea shop using Microsoft Excel. The aim is to demonstrate how basic Excel functions can help evaluate sales trends and support business planning.

-Body

The dataset contains three months of transaction records, including store location, product type, unit price, quantity, and total revenue. To analyse this data, I used several Excel functions. First, I calculated the total revenue with =SUM(F:F). This provides an overview of the overall business performance. Then, I applied =SUMIFS(F:F, B:B, "Auckland") to evaluate the revenue generated specifically by the Auckland store. This formula helps identify which location is most profitable. Furthermore, I used =COUNTIFS(C:C, "Pearl Milk Tea") to count how many orders were for the best-selling product. This information is valuable for inventory management. Finally, I used VLOOKUP to match product names with their prices, which improves data accuracy and reduces manual errors.

-Conclusion

In conclusion, Excel is a powerful tool for business analytics. It allows users to transform raw data into meaningful insights that support decision-making. Through this analysis, I learned how to use SUMIFS, COUNTIFS, and VLOOKUP to solve practical business problems. These skills will be highly relevant for my future studies in business and economics, and they represent a significant step toward becoming a data-driven professional.
