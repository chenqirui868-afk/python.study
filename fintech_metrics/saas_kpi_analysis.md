# Key FinTech Metrics: CAC, LTV, and Payback Period

## 1. Definitions

| Metric | Definition | Formula |
|--------|------------|---------|
| **CAC** (Customer Acquisition Cost) | Total sales & marketing cost divided by number of new customers acquired in a period. | Total market spend/the number of new customers |
| **LTV** (Lifetime Value) | Total revenue a business can expect from a single customer over the entire relationship. | Average Monthly Revenue per Users * Customer Lifespan(months) |
| **Payback Period** | Months needed to recover the CAC from a customer's gross margin contribution. |CAC/(Average Monthly Revenue per Users * The Rate of Proifile)|

## 3. A Real-World Scenario (Example)

Assume a neo-bank spends **$50,000** on ads and acquires **1,000** new users.

- CAC = [50,000/1,000 = $50]
- If each user generates **$15/month** and stays **24 months**:
  - LTV = [15 * 24 = 360]
- Payback Period = [50/(15 * 60%) = 5.56(about 5.5 months)]
